# Delete your {{app_name}} account

You can delete your {{app_name}} account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

## Option 1 — In the app

Open the app, go to **Profile → Delete account**, and confirm with the one-time password sent to your mobile number.

## Option 2 — Request by email

Send an email to **{{contact_email}}** with the subject **"Delete my account"** and include:

- the **mobile number** registered with {{app_name}}, and
- your **name** as entered in the app.

We will confirm the request by sending an OTP or message to your registered mobile number, so that nobody else can delete your account.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: mobile number, name, email and state.
- Your sign-in sessions on all devices (you are signed out everywhere).
- Pending one-time passwords.

Deletion is completed within **{{deletion_days}} days**. Limited information may be kept only where the law requires it, for no longer than required.

## What is not affected

Videos and documents published in the app are not personal data and are not affected.
